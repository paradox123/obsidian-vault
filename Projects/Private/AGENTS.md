# Private Project Context

## What is this project about?

Private is Daniel Hecht's personal knowledge and operations area. It covers
paperwork and correspondence, taxes, property and rental administration,
energetic renovation of the family home, personal planning, professional
profile and applications, portfolio material, and local AI-assisted
workflows.

The main long-running property project is the 2025-2028 energetic renovation
of the family's listed former-estate residence in Schlüchtern-Vollmerz.
Private is nevertheless an umbrella project: a meeting may concern
renovation, rental management, personal administration, career work, finance,
or another clearly personal topic.

There is no single fixed delivery team for all Private topics.

  primary coordinator and decision-maker for personal projects.
- Daniel, Samantha, Maxine, and Mika Hecht are the documented family
  household associated with the property and renovation context.

Do not treat every person in the private notes as a project-team member.
Derive participants from the current meeting and the relevant subproject's
contact or source note. Avoid repeating personal contact details unless they
are necessary for the requested artifact.

## What is Daniel's position in the project?

Daniel is the owner, maintainer, and primary decision-maker for the Private
knowledge area. Depending on the subproject, he acts as:

- property owner and renovation coordinator;
- landlord and private administrator;
- owner of personal finance and paperwork workflows;
- owner of his professional profile, applications, and acquisition process;
- solution architect and platform engineer for his internal Shared AI
  Platform and DanielsVault workflows.

For renovation work, Daniel coordinates documentation, contractors,
authorities, funding, financing, procurement, and parts of the owner-provided
work. Do not infer that Daniel is the licensed specialist, legal adviser,
energy adviser, or executing contractor.
